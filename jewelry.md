
# Jewelry
Jewelry allows a character to add passive effects while not wearing [armor](armor.md).

Each standard piece of jewelry may accept up to one [enchantment](TODO.md).

## Amulets
You may wear up to *one* amulet regardless of your equipped armor.

## Circlets
You may wear up to *one* circlet if the Armor Class for your Helm is `Unarmored`.

## Rings
You may wear up to *two* rings if the Armor Class for your Gloves is `Unarmored`.
