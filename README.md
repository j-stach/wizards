
# WIZARDS
The WIZARDS System is a perennial TTRPG framework that can be adapted to a variety of settings and formats, 
eliminating the need to monitor various ever-changing rules in order to enjoy tabletop wargaming.

The goal of this system is to provide a gameplay experience that is easy to learn and remember,
while providing mechanical depth that supports a wide variety of niche charater types.

WIZARDS is named after its Character Attribute system, 
which uses the memorable acronym [W.I.Z.A.R.D.S.](/rules/character/attributes.md). 


## Rolling Dice
[Dice](/rules/core/dice.md) are used to determine the outcome of events.

To make the dice mechanics easier to learn and remember, 
the WIZARDS system assigns a specific role for each die in the standard TTRPG set.

This also makes abilities more balanced and combat more consistent. 


## Tabletop Combat
WIZARDS was created to simplify Tabletop [combat](/rules/core/combat.md) using miniatures, 
and some rules may rely on concepts like "Line-of-Sight" and "Base Contact" 
that may not translate well to theater-of-mind approaches to roleplaying.

This system is rules-driven and designed to remove as much interpretation from combat as possible
so that the *Dicemaster* (DM) is never overburdened.

In an adventuring campaign setting, 
the DM may control the actions of antagonistic forces and "Non-Player Characters",
but from the perspective of the rules, the DM is mostly treated as "just another player."

This scalable approach supports a variety of game formats including 
squadron-based missions and even full-scale wargaming,
with or without a DM.


## Character Progression
Character [creation](/rules/character/new_character.md) 
and [progression](/rules/character/progression/level-up.md) are open and flexible.

Characters may use passive [perks](/rules/character/progression/perks.md),
action [abilities](/rules/character/progression/abilities.md),
and equipment such as [weapons](/rules/equipment/items/weapons.md)
if they meet the WIZARDS [attributes](/rules/character/attributes.md) requirements.

[Classes](/rules/character/progression/classes/) are prerequisite trees of perks and abilities
that conform to an adventuring archetype and combat role, 
but characters are not limited to only one, nor are they required to progress in a class at all.


## Worldbuilding
This repository provides a set of core rules and guidelines that are designed 
to be flexibly adapted or expanded to suit the world you are building for your game scenarios.

This includes [playable races](/rules/world/race.md),
exchangeable [currency](/rules/world/currency.md) and economies,
[factions](/rules/world/factions.md) with loyalty mechanics,
[inhabitable towns](/rules/world/towns.md) and other locations,
routes for [traveling](/rules/world/traveling.md),
and [elemental systems](/rules/world/elements.md) that infuse spells and hazards.

These core mechanics follow principles that guide worldbuilding in a balanced way.
