
# Standard Potions 
"Charges" refers to the number of charges that can fit in a [Flask](/equipment/items/standard.md).

## Potions
|Name|Charges|Effect|
|----|-------|------|
|Healing|5|For your next `d10` actions, you may restore `d4` life as part of that action.|
|Mana|5|For your next `d10` actions, you may gain `1` mana as part of that action.|
|Estus|2|Restore `20` life.|
