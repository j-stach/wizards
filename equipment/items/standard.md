
# Standard Items

## Liquid Measurement
|Name|Storage Class|# Hands|Description|
|----|-------------|-------|-----------|
|Flask|Small|1|Vessel for [potions](potions.md). Can only contain one kind of potion at a time. 
As an action, you may consume 1 charge from an equipped flask to activate the potion's effect.|
|Bottle|Medium|1|Vessel for potions. Can only contain one kind of potion at a time. 
Contains up to twice the number of charges in a basic flask.
As an action, you may consume 1 charge from an equipped bottle to activate the potion's effect.|
|Jug|Large|2|Vessel for potions. Can only contain one kind of potion at a time. 
Contains up to twice the number of charges in a basic bottle (four flasks).
As an action, you may consume 1 charge from an equipped jug to activate the potion's effect.|
