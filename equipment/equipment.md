
# Equipment
Equipment that is worn by the character is called *apparel*,
while equipment that are hand-held and used with actions are *items*.

### Apparel
In addition to [armor](/equipment/apparel/armor.md), 
characters can equip [accessories](/equipment/apparel/accessories.md), 
and [storage](/equipment/apparel/storage.md) for inventory.

### Items
Basic items that are common to all campaigns include archetypal [weapons](/equipment/items/weapons.md),
simple potions [potions](/equipment/items/potions.md),
and [standard](/equipment/items/standard.md) adventuring items to be used for measurement.
