
# Potions & Poisons
"Charges" refers to the number of charges that can fit in a Flask (see [items](items.md))

## Potions
|Name|Charges|Effect|
|----|-------|------|
|Healing|5|For your next `d10` actions, you may restore `d4` life as part of that action.|
|Mana|5|For your next `d10` actions, you may gain `1` mana as part of that action.|
|Estus|2|Restore `20` life.|

## Poisons
|Name|Charges|Effect|
|----|-------|------|
|Feebleness|5|For the next `d10` actions, the target loses advantage on all Clash (d6) rolls.|
|Sluggishness|5|For the next `d10` actions, the target's maximum Move distance becomes `1"`.|
|Decay|5|For the next `d10` actions, the target may choose to do nothing instead. If the target makes an action, it takes `d4` damage.|
