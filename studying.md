
# Studying
Studying lore allows players to learn knowledge about the world.

If a character can study lore, 
they may choose one of the effects below whenever they level up in a town with a *Library*.

|Name|Effect|
|----|------|
|Reflection|The DM tells the player about any *clues* the party missed during the previous quest|
|Preparation|Gain advantage on all **Narrative** rolls for Wisdom & Intelligence until the next time you level-up|
|Exploration|Roll for **Outcome** on the Library's "hidden lore" discovery table, if it has one|
|Information|Choose a topic and roll for **Narrative**. The DM tells the player information about the chosen topic.|
