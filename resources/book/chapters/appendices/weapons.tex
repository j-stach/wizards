\section{Melee Weapons}

\subsection{Blades}

\begin{Table}{l c c c c c}
  \TableHeader{Weapon} & \TableHeader{WIZARDS} & \TableHeader{Size} & \TableHeader{Hands} & \TableHeader{Range} & \TableHeader{Damage} \\

  Short Sword & a5, d5 & Large & 1 & Base & 1d8 \\

\section{Shields}
Carrying a Shield in one hand grants the corresponding \textit{Raise Shield} ability.

\begin{Table}{l c c X}
  \TableHeader{Shield} & \TableHeader{WIZARDS} & \TableHeader{Size} & \TableHeader{Raise Shield} \\
  Buckler & a5/d5 & Large & \textbf{Reaction:} Gain +1 on your next Clash Save \\
  Round Shield & a5, r5 & Attach & Gain +1 on Clash Saves until your next turn \\
  Tower Shield & r5, s5 & N/A & Gain Cover from the front until your next turn \\
\end{Table}

\section{Mana Focuses}
The \textit{Range} value limits the \textbf{maximum} range for spells cast using the focus.

If the range is ``N/A", the spell is only limited by its own range.

\vspace{0.4em}

The \textit{Damage} value is the default damage die used for spells cast using the focus.

If a spell does not specify the die type used for the spell damage (i.e., 1dD),
then the spell uses this die type.

\begin{Table}{l c c c c c}
  \TableHeader{Weapon} & \TableHeader{WIZARDS} & \TableHeader{Size} & \TableHeader{Hands} & \TableHeader{Range} & \TableHeader{Damage} \\
  Talisman & w5/i5/z5 & Small & 1 & 6" & d4 \\
  Wand & z5, w5/i5 & Medium & 1 & 12" & d8 \\
  Staff & w5, i5/z5 & N/A & 1/2 & 24"/N/A & d8/d12 \\
  Tome & i5, w5/z5 & Medium & 2 & N/A & d8 \\
\end{Table}
