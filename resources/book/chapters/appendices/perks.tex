\section{Combat Proficiency}

\subsection{Weapon Proficiency}

\begin{Table}{l c X}
  \TableHeader{Perk} & \TableHeader{WIZARDS} & \TableHeader{Effect} \\
  Polearms & w5 & Gain +1 to all rolls for abilities using Polearm weapons \\
  Blades   & a5 & Gain +1 to all rolls for abilities using Bladed weapons \\
  Axes     & r5 & Gain +1 to all rolls for abilities using Axe weapons \\
  Hammers  & s5 & Gain +1 to all rolls for abilities using Hammer weapons \\
  Archery  & d5 & Gain +1 to all rolls for abilities using Archery weapons \\
  Firearm  & d5 & Gain +1 to all rolls for abilities using Firearm weapons \\
\end{Table}

\subsection{Enchanting}
\textit{Enchanting} perks modify how a character improves items using magic.

\begin{Table}{l c c X}
  \TableHeader{Perk} & \TableHeader{WIZARDS} & \TableHeader{Prerequisites} & \TableHeader{Effect} \\
  Enchanted Weapons & i5, d5 & N/A & \textbf{Skill:} You may enchant weapons \& items \\
  Enchanted Apparel & i5, d5 & N/A & \textbf{Skill:} You may enchant armor \& accessories \\
  Magical Weapons   & w7 & Enchanted Weapons & When you enchant weapons \& rings, you may instead imbue them with a spell that has a range of Base. Whenever attacks using the hand equipped with the weapon or ring successfully hit, you may pay any costs associated with the spell to trigger the spell effect on the target as part of the weapon attack effect. \\
  Magical Apparel   & w7 & Enchanted Apparel & When you enchant armor \& accessories, you may instead imbue them with a spell that has a range of Self. When you are hit by an attack, you may pay any costs associated with the spell to trigger the spell effect. \\  
  Infused Enchantments & w10 & N/A & When you enchant items, you may instead imbue them with a Reservoir (i.e, Life or Mana). The base value for the reservoir is equal to \Roll{1d10} + your character's \textbf{Intelligence} and \textbf{Zest} modifiers. Reservoirs imbued in weapons \& rings may be spent to activate abilities of that weapon, or of the item equipped in the hand that is equipped with the ring. Reservoirs imbued in armor \& accessories may be freely spent in place of the character's reservoirs. When a character's reservoirs are restored by a spell, they may choose to restore the reservoir of their equipment instead. \\
\end{Table}
