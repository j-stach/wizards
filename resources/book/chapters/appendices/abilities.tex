\chapter{Abilities}
These abilities can be taken by any character who meets the W.I.Z.A.R.D.S. requirements.

\begin{Table}{l c c X}
  \TableHeader{Ability} & \TableHeader{WIZARDS}  & \TableHeader{Range} & \TableHeader{Effect} \\
  Perceive & w5 & 12" & Learn the properties of your environment. If there is a ``discovery" table, you may roll \Roll{1d10} for a guaranteed outcome. \\
  Scrutinize & w5 & 6" & Learn the properties of an NPC. If the character has a ``discovery" table, you may roll \Roll{1d10} for a guaranteed outcome. \\
  Inspect & w5, i5 & Base & Learn an object's properties. If the object has a ``discovery" table, you may roll \Roll{1d10} for a guaranteed outcome. \\
  Refocus & w5, z5 & Self & Roll \Roll{1d10}: On a 10+, remove all effects of Charm and Persuade. This action is repeatable. \\
  Dodge & w5, a5 & Self & You gain 1 re-roll that may be used on any Clash Save made before the start of your next turn. \\
  Conserve & w5, r5 & Self & If your next action would consume resources from a pool (e.g., mana), it consumes one less instead (to a minimum of 1 Mana). \\
  Caution & w5, d5 & 6" & Target within 6" gains 1 re-roll that may be used on any \Roll{d10} roll made before the start of your next turn. \\
  Intervene & w5, s5 & 1" & \textbf{Reaction:} If an ability targets an ally within 1", you may have that ability target you instead. \\
  Analyze & i5 & 6" & Choose a target character or object and roll \Roll{1d10}: On a 10+, gain +1 to all rolls against the target for \Roll{1d10} turns. \\
  Predict & i5 & Self & Gain an additional reaction until the start of your next turn \\
  Distract & i5, z5 & 1" & Target enemy within 1" cannot use reactions against your allies until the start of your next turn. Trigger Flanking as if dealing damage to the target. \\
  Disengage & i5, a5 & Base & Enemies within base contact cannot use reactions against you until the start of your next turn. \\
  Persist & i5, r5 & Self & If your previous action was not an attack or movement action, repeat it. \\
  Repair & i5, d5 & Base & Roll \Roll{1d10}: On a 10+, a broken object becomes usable. This action is repeatable. \\
  Leverage & i5, s5 & Self & Your next action this turn gains advantage on Clash rolls. \\
  Charm & z5 & 2" & Clash with a target within 2": If you win, the target improves it's allegiance to you (i.e., Hostile \rightarrow~ Neutral \rightarrow~ Ally), and you gain advantage on Clash rolls against that target. This effect lasts for \Roll{1d10} turns. If you lose, the target becomes immune to your Charm. \\
  Persuade & z5 & 2" (6") & Clash with a target within 2": If you win, you may change the allegiance of the target relative to another single target within 6" (e.g., Hostile \rightarrow~ Neutral, Ally \rightarrow~ Hostile, etc.). This effect lasts for \Roll{1d10} turns. If you lose, all future rolls you make to Persuade the target lose advantage. \\
  Weave & z5, a5 & Self & Your next Move action may pass through enemy bases, as long as you do not end the movement on an enemy base. \\
  Recalcitrate & z5, r5 & Self & You gain re-rolls on all Clash Saves against abilities with a Zest requirement until the start of your next turn. \\
  Flourish & z5, d5 & Self & You gain a re-roll on the next Clash roll you make this turn. \\
  Flex & z5, s5 & Self & You gain a re-roll on the next Damage roll you make this turn. \\
  Leap & a5 & Self & The path of your next Move action ignores up to 2" of continuous hazardous terrain, but may not end in hazardous terrain. \\
  Dash & a5 & Self & Move up to your maximum move distance, as though you were making a Move action. \\
  Sprint & a5, r5 & Self & Your maximum move distance is doubled for the next Move action you make this turn. The path must be a straight line, and it cannot bring you within 1" of any enemies. \\
\end{Table}

\begin{Table}{l c c X}
  \TableHeader{Ability} & \TableHeader{WIZARDS}  & \TableHeader{Range} & \TableHeader{Effect} \\
  Intercept & a5, d5 & 1" & Requires 1 free hand. If a non-magical projectile's path crosses within 1", you may roll \textbf{d10}: On a 10+, move into the path of the projectile and equip it in your free hand. \\
  Push & a5, s5 & Base & Clash (d6) with a character that is in base contact and is Similar Size or Smaller. If you win, the target staggers up to 1" radially away from you; On a Critical Roll, the target staggers up to 1" and is knocked prone, taking Light (\Roll{d4}) fall damage, then if the target's path of movement during stagger crosses the base of another unit, you may have the target make a free Push action against the other unit. This free Push loses Advantage. \\
  Brace & r5 & Self & The next Damage roll made against you this turn loses Advantage. \\
  Recover & r5 & Self & The next Restoration roll that would heal you this turn gains Advantage. \\
  Conceal & r5, d5 & Self & Until the start of your next turn, if a Perceive, Scrutinize, Inspect, Analyze, or Predict action would target you (or an object in your possession), you may force the character performing the action to Clash with you. If you win, the action fails entirely, and if you lose, the action succeeds but loses advantage on Outcome (\Roll{d10}) rolls. \\
  Invigorate & r5, s5 & Self & \textbf{Reaction:} Gain advantage on Outcome (\Roll{d10}) rolls to remain conscious. \\
  Steady & d5 & Self & \textbf{Reaction:} If you would fall from a ledge, roll \Roll{d10}: On a 3+ you remain standing; On a 2, you grab the ledge; On a 1, you continue to fall. \\
  Throw & d5 & 6" & Use a Tiny, Small, or Medium item, or a one-hand weapon as a Light (\Roll{d4}) projectile. If you throw a weapon, you may make a ranged attack with that weapon instead. \\
  Hurl & d5, s5 & 4" & Use a Medium or Large item, or a one- or two-hand weapon as a Normal (\Roll{d8}) projectile. If you throw a weapon, you may make a ranged attack with that weapon instead. \\
  Heave & s5 & 2" & Use a Large item, or a two-hand weapon as a Heavy (\Roll{d12}) projectile. If you throw a weapon, you may make a ranged attack with that weapon instead. \\
  Slam & s5 & Self & Deal Heavy (\Roll{d12}) damage to a target that you have equipped via the Grapple ability. The target may make a free Grapple action. \\
\end{Table}
