
\chapter{Perks, Abilities, and Classes}


\section{Perks}
Perks are passive effects that apply to your character at all times.

\vspace{0.4em}

Unless another perk is mentioned as a prerequisite, 
a character only needs to meet the W.I.Z.A.R.D.S. requirements 
in order to add a Perk when they level-up.

\section{Abilities}

Abilities provide an Action that the Character may use on their turn during combat.

\vspace{0.4em}

Additional abilities may added that are specific to a campaign/setting, 
but the Core abilities from Chapter 3: Combat and Appendix B: Abilities 
(and those in the archetypal classes in Appendix C: Character Classes)
can be taken in any campaign.
