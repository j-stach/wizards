
\chapter{Equipment}

\section{Weapons}
Weapons can be used by any character.
If a character does not meet the WIZARDS requirement for a weapon they are using, 
they lose advantage on all actions made using that weapon.

\vspace{0.4em}

If a weapon size is "N/A", then it can only be stored using an attachment point.

\vspace{0.4em}

Weapons can be [improved](/rules/character/progression/skills/crafting.md).

\subsection{Shield}
Carrying a Shield in one hand grants the corresponding Raise Shield ability.

\subsection{Projectile}
Archery weapons require *Arrow* ammunition.
The base arrow deals `d8` damage.

\vspace{0.4em}

Firearm weapons require a *Round* ammunition that is specific to the Firearm type.
Each firearm can hold up to its capacity in rounds, 
and can expend a round to make a projectile attack.
When a firearm holds no rounds, you may use an action to reload the firearm.

\subsection{Mana Focus}
A character equipped with a mana focus may spend mana to cast [spells](/rules/magic/spells.md).

\vspace{0.4em}

**Range** limits the maximum range for spells cast using the focus.
If the range is "N/A", the spell is only limited by its own range.

\vspace{0.4em}

**Damage** is the default spell damage die used for spells cast using the focus.

\subsection{Amulets}
You may wear up to 1 amulet regardless of your armor.

\subsection{Circlets}
You may wear up to 1 circlet if the Armor Class for your Helm is Unarmored.

\subsection{Rings}
You may wear up to 2 rings if the Armor Class for your Gloves is Unarmored or Light.
