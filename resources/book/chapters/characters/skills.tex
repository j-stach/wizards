\section{Studying}
Studying lore allows players to safely gain knowledge about the world.

If a character can [study lore](/rules/character/progression/perks.md), 
they may choose one of the effects below whenever they [level-up](/rules/character/progression/level-up.md) 
in a [town](/rules/world/towns.md) with a *Library*.

|Name|Effect|
|----|------|
|Reflection|Roll for **Narrative** and add your Intelligence modifier. The DM tells the player about any environmental *clues* you missed since the last time you leveled-up.|
|Preparation|Gain advantage on all **Narrative** rolls for Wisdom \& Intelligence until the next time you level-up.|
|Exploration|Roll on the Library's "hidden lore" discovery table (d10/d100), if it has one.|
|Edification|Choose a topic and roll for **Narrative**, adding your Intelligence modifier. The DM tells the player information about the chosen topic.|



\section{Writing}
Players can create notes at any time for their own rememberance, 
but these are not in-game items that can be passed on to new characters.

Writing exists for the easy transferral of information between characters,
and mostly consists of collecting useful knowledge into one place,
but also can record unique character experiences and [crafted spells](/rules/character/progression/skills/spellcrafting.md).

When a player [levels-up](/rules/character/progression/level-up.md) 
in a [town](/rules/world/towns.md) with a *Library*, they may choose 1:
- if the player can [inscribe](/rules/character/progression/perks.md), they may create 2 folios or 1 scrolls, or
- if the player can [map](/rules/character/progression/perks.md), they may create 1 map.

\subsection{Tomes \& Scrolls}
*Tomes* are comprised of a variable number of *folios* (pages) that can contain information.
Tomes begin with a number of blank folios, which may be inscribed.
The [storage class](/rules/equipment/apparel/storage.md) of a tome depends on the maximum number of 
folios it is able to contain.

|No. Folios|Storage Class|
|--------|-------------|
|Up to 10|Medium|
|Greater than 10|Large|

*Scrolls* are created as independent items that are treated as if they were a tome of 2 folios.

|Information Type|No. Folios|Description|
|----------------|--------|-----------|
|Spell|1|Teach the procedure for casting a [spell](/rules/magic/spells.md)|
|Fact|1|Record an important plot point or fact of lore, and all essential context to understand that fact|
|Illustration|1/2|Visually depict a character, item, flora or fauna in a portrait or diagram (1 folio) or a landscape from a vantage point you have visited (2 folios)| 


\subsection{Maps}
*Maps* are used to record the geographic layout of a location or region, 
and may contain all spatial facts a player would require to physically navigate the depicted region, 
including the placement of sublocations, environmental hazards, and other points of interest.

Maps are treated as if they were 2 folios, 
and may be incorporated into a tome or created independently as a scroll.



\section{Crafting}
Crafting skill allows characters to *create* or *improve* [weapons](/rules/equipment/items/weapons.md) 
and [armor](/rules/equipment/apparel/armor.md).

If a character can craft weapons and armor, 
they may craft or improve *one* weapon or armor piece whenever they 
[level-up](/rules/character/progression/level-up.md) in a [town](/rules/world/towns.md) with a *Workshop*.

\subsection{Crafting Cost}
When you create or improve a weapon, you must expend a number of metal ingots:
|Hands|Ingots|
|-------|--------|
| 1 | 1 |
|1/2| 2 |
| 2 | 3 |

When you create or improve armor, you must expend a number of metal ingots and/or textiles:
|Armor Class|Ingots|Textiles|
|-----------|--------|-------|
|Unarmored| 0 | 2 |
|Light| 1 | 1 |
|Heavy| 2 | 0 |


\subsection{Improvement}
Improvements are effects that are added to weapons or armor through crafting 
or [enchantment](TODO.md), and are often campaign-specific.

Standard weapons and armor may have up to `2` improvements.
Each item of equipment can only have one improvement with a given name.

These improvements can be added to a weapon in any campaign.

|Name|Effect|
|----|------|
|Intricate|Add `+2` to the maximum number of improvements for this weapon|
|Lightweight|All WIZARDS requirements for this weapon can be satisfied with Dexterity|
|Honed|Add `+1` to all **Damage** rolls for attacks using this weapon|

These improvements can be added to armor in any campaign.

|Name|Effect|
|----|------|
|Intricate|Add `+2` to the maximum number of improvements for this weapon|
|Fitted|Reduce the WIZARDS attribute penalty by `1`, if possible (e.g. `-2` -> `-1`, `-1` -> `0`)|
|Lightweight|Decrease the armor class of this armor by one, if possible (Heavy -> Light, Light -> Unarmored)|
|Reinforced|Increase the armor class of this armor by one, if possible (Unarmored -> Light, Light -> Heavy)|



\section{Arcana} % Spellcrafting
Spellcrafting allows characters to create new spells by synthesizing two spells into a single effect.

If a character can [spellcraft](/rules/character/progression/perks.md), 
they may craft one spell whenever they 
[level-up](/rules/character/progression/level-up.md) in a [town](/rules/world/towns.md) with an *Arcaneum*.

Choose two [spells](/rules/magic/spells.md) that you already know and wish to combine. 
Roll a `d10` to determine the outcome:

|Roll|Outcome|
|----|-------|
|1-2|You immediately suffer the effects of both spells as if you were the target of those spells.|
|3-4|Nothing happens.|
|5-8|Gain advantage the next time you craft the same two spells.|
|9-10|Combine the effects and costs of the spells into a new spell, and give it a name. Its range becomes the lesser range of the two spells. You learn that spell.|
