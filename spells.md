
# Spells
Characters must equip a *spell focus* (e.g., wand, staff) to cast spells.

## Basic Spells
These spells can be taken by any character.

|Spell|Range|Mana Cost|Effect|
|-----|-----|---------|-----|
|Heal| Base | 0 | Target within base contact restores 1d8 health. Pay an additional X mana to increase the range by X".|
|Ward| Self | 0 | Gain +1 to Clash Saves until your next turn. Pay an additional X mana to target another character, and increase the range by X".|
|Bolt| 6" | 1 | Make a ranged spell attack on a target within 6". Deals 1d12 damage if using a two-handed focus, or 1d8 otherwise. |
|Siphon| 6" | 0 | Make a ranged spell attack against a target within 6". Instead of dealing damage to health, reduce the target's mana by 1d8 if using a two-handed focus, or by 1d4 otherwise, and you restore that much mana (up to your maximum mana).|
|Counter| Self | X | When you are targeted by a spell, you may use your reaction to pay X, where X is the amount of mana the caster spent on the spell. If you do, Clash (d6) with the caster. If you win, the spell loses all effects, otherwise the caster loses advantage on all rolls for that spell.|
|Focus| Self | 0 | Gain advantage on the next Damage roll (d4/d8/d12) you make for a spell. If you move, this advantage is lost.|
|Burst| Self | X | Deal `Xd4` damage to **all** characters within `X"` radius, other than yourself.|
