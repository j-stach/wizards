## Amenities
Amenities enable certain passive abilities when you [level-up](/character/progression/level-up.md) in a town that provides them:

|Amenity|Purpose|
|-------|-------|
|Workshop|[Crafting](/character/progression/skills/crafting.md) *non-magical* weapons & armor|
|Library|[Studying](/character/progression/skills/studying.md) & crafting books & maps|
|Apothecary|Identifying materials & crafting [potions](/equipment/items/potions.md)|
|Arcaneum|[Spellcrafting](/character/progression/skills/spellcrafting.md)|
|Hospital|Healing diseases & curses|
