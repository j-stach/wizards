## Amenities
Certain passive abilities (skills) may be triggered when you [level-up](/character/progression/level-up.md) in a town that provides a compatible amenity:

|Amenity|Associated Skill(s)|
|-------|-------|
|Workshop|[Crafting](/character/progression/skills/crafting.md) weapons & armor|
|Library|[Studying](/character/progression/skills/studying.md) and [writing](/character/progression/skills/writing.md)|
|Apothecary|Analyzing materials & crafting [potions](/equipment/items/potions.md)|
|Arcaneum|[Spellcrafting](/character/progression/skills/spellcrafting.md) and [enchantment](TODO.md)|
|Hospital|Healing [wounds](TODO.md) & [afflictions](TODO.md)|
|Temple|Prayer and [blessings](TODO.md)|
