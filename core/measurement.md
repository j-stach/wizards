
# Measurement

## Distance
Distance is measured in inches, with `1"` roughly corresponding to two paces.

## Volume
[Volume](/equipment/items/standard.md) is measured in the context of 
inventory [storage](/equipment/apparel/storage.md)
and liquid [potions](/equipment/items/potions.md).

## Weapon Size
[Weapon](/equipment/items/weapons.md) size is determined by how many hands the weapon takes to wield.

## Character/Creature Size
A character's size is determined by their [race](/world/race.md).

Character sizes are approximate in a wide general range 
and considered *Similarly-sized* when they are in the same overall class. 

Character/creature size classes:
|Size|Example|
|----|-------------------|
|Tiny| Bug, Rodent, Pixie |
|Small| Cat, Dog, Gnome |
|Normal| Dwarf, Human, Wolf |
|Large| Horse, Ogre, Giant, Automobile |
|Enormous| Elephant, Wyvern, House, Kraken|
|Titanic| Leviathan, Elder Dragon, Kaiju|
