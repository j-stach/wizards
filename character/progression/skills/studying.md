
# Studying
Studying lore allows players to safely gain knowledge about the world.

If a character can [study lore](/character/progression/perks.md), 
they may choose one of the effects below whenever they [level-up](/character/progression/level-up.md) 
in a [town](/world/towns.md) with a *Library*.

|Name|Effect|
|----|------|
|Reflection|Roll for **Narrative** and add your Intelligence modifier. The DM tells the player about any environmental *clues* you missed since the last time you leveled-up.|
|Preparation|Gain advantage on all **Narrative** rolls for Wisdom & Intelligence until the next time you level-up.|
|Exploration|Roll on the Library's "hidden lore" discovery table (d10/d100), if it has one.|
|Edification|Choose a topic and roll for **Narrative**, adding your Intelligence modifier. The DM tells the player information about the chosen topic.|
