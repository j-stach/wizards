
# Jewelry
Each standard piece of jewelry may accept up to one [enchantment](TODO.md).

### Amulets
You may wear up to *one* amulet regardless of your [armor](/rules/equipment/apparel/armor.md).

### Circlets
You may wear up to *one* circlet if the Armor Class for your Helm is `Unarmored`.

### Rings
You may wear up to *two* rings if the Armor Class for your Gloves is `Unarmored` or `Light`.
