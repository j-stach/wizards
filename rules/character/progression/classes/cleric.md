
# Cleric
The *Cleric* relies on their patron's blessings to support allies and hinder enemies.


## Perks

### Healer
|Perk|WIZARDS|Prerequisites|Effect|
|----|-------|-------------|------|
|Healer|w7|N/A|Gain advantage on all rolls for abilities that restore or gain Health and Mana|
|Restorer|N/A|Healer, Restoration|Gain `+1` to all rolls for abilities that restore or gain Health or Mana|
|Remote Restoration|N/A|Healer, Intervene|Gain `+1"` to the range for abilities that restore or gain Health and Mana|

### Blesser
|Perk|WIZARDS|Prerequisites|Effect|
|----|-------|-------------|------|
|Blesser|z7|N/A|Abilities with a range of `Self` may be used as if they had a range of `Base`|
|Supporter|N/A|Blesser, Caution|Allies within `1"` gain `+2` to all outcome (d10) rolls|
|Free Charity|N/A|Blesser, Conserve|Spells that target allies cost `1` less Mana to use (to a minimum of 1 Mana)|

|Healing Echo|d7|Healer|Self|The next time you use an ability that restores or gains Health or Mana this round, you may repeat the ability for a different target, using the same results from all rolls from the previous use. (You must pay all costs associated with the ability.)|
