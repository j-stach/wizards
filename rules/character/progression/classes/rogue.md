
# Rogue
The *Rogue* uses finesse and stealth to exploit weaknesses in combat.


## Perks

### Thief
|Perk|WIZARDS|Prerequisites|Effect|
|----|-------|-------------|------|
|Thief|d7|N/A|You may re-roll all rolls when using the Equip and Interact actions|
|Trickster|N/A|Thief, Distract|When you use `Distract` against a target within base contact, you also gain [Flanking](/rules/core/combat.md) against that target|
|Shadow|N/A|Thief, Conceal|When you gain [Flanking](/rules/core/combat.md) on a target within base contact, you may use `Conceal` as a free action. Whenever you succeed on a `Conceal` action, you also gain [Cover](/rules/core/combat.md) against all characters until the start of your next turn.|

### Assassin
|Perk|WIZARDS|Prerequisites|Effect|
|----|-------|-------------|------|
|Assassin|i7|N/A|Your attacks gain `+1` to all rolls for which you also have advantage|
|Serpent|N/A|Assassin, Weave|When you pass through an enemy base while using `Weave`, gain advantage on all rolls for the next attack you make against that enemy this turn|
|Scorpion|N/A|Assassin, Intercept|Whenever you successfully `Intercept` a projectile, you may immediately make a free attack action using that projectile, and this attack gains advantage on Clash (d6) rolls|
