
# Elemental System
Some [spells](/rules/magic/spells.md) and [abilities](/rules/character/pregression/abilities.md)
may require the user to choose an *Element* as part of its effect.

Each Element modifies the selected ability in a way that reflects the physical phenomenon it represents.
Elemental systems can be expanded to suit the physics of your campaign.

|Element|Effect|
|-------|------|
|Shock| Damage from this ability may damage the target's Mana instead of Life.
If a **Shock** attack would damage Mana, it must be declared before damage dice are rolled.
Critical damage that is dealt to Mana *electrocutes* the target for `1d10` turns.
While a character is electrocuted, they take `1d4` damage to their Mana at the beginning of their turn.|
|Fire| Critical damage *ignites* the target for `1d10` turns. 
While a character is ignited, they take `1d4` damage at the beginning of their turn.
While ignited, a character may spend 3 Actions to extinguish the fire, removing the condition.
If an ignited character takes **Frost** elemental damage, the fire is extinguished, removing the condition.|
|Frost| Critical damage reduces the target's Move distance by `1"` until the start of their next turn.
If this reduces a character's Move distance to `0"`, then that character become *frozen* for `1d10` turns. 
While a character is frozen, they are [restrained](TODO.md) and cannot use actions.
If a frozen character would take **Fire** elemental damage, 
that damage is prevented and instead the ice is thawed, removing the condition.|
